About me.

Software engineer, game developer, and fly fisherman in Michigan.

Background

I’m interested in what happens after someone presses a button.

In a maintenance app, that means a repair note has to reach the next technician. In a co-op game, it means two players opening the same door should see the same thing. Those are the kinds of details I enjoy working through.

I graduated from Oakland University in May 2026 with a B.S. in Computer Science. My projects span mobile interfaces, Python APIs, and multiplayer game systems.
