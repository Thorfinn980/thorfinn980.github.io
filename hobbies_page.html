About me.

Software engineer, game developer, and fly fisherman.

Background

I get obsessive about the details that make an experience feel real.

Fly fishing shapes the simulations I want to build. What makes one fish peel away from a school to follow a lure while the others swim past? I want to recreate the behavior of the animals I watch on the water as faithfully as I can, down to the moment a fish loses interest and turns away.

I'm a recent Oakland University graduate. I earned my B.S. in Computer Science and walked at commencement in May 2026.

I also like finding out how far I can push a computer. For GPU Blur, I timed the same image filter on the CPU and GPU to see what I gained by processing pixels in parallel. I enjoy hunting for the next bottleneck and measuring whether a change actually helped.
