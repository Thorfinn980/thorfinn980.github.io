About me.

Software engineer, game developer, and fly fisherman.

Background

I earned my B.S. in Computer Science at Oakland University and walked at commencement in May 2026.

In my spare time, I make games and write simulations. Fly fishing gives me a lot of ideas. When I see a fish holding its place in a fast current, I want to see if I can get that movement right on screen.

I enjoy the performance side, too. Once a school of fish moves the way I want, I start wondering how many more my GPU can handle before the frame rate drops.
